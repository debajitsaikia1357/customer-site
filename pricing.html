Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about categories, billing, and transitioning to HRGenX.

Can we start with Category 1 (Essentials) and upgrade later?
Yes, absolutely. HRGenX is built on a modular unified data architecture. All employee records, histories, and org configurations carry over seamlessly the moment you activate Enablement or Enterprise 3E.
Are there any hidden setup or implementation fees?
No hidden fees. Starter Essentials and Enablement tiers include self-guided onboarding templates and automated CSV migration tools at zero additional charge. Tailored white-glove migration for 500+ employee enterprises is scoped transparently upfront.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
You get complete, unrestricted access to the category of your choice for 14 days. No credit card is required to sign up. You can invite your team, test attendance geo-fencing, and run sandbox payroll dry-runs.
Do you accommodate multi-state and international payroll rules?
Yes. HRGenX automatically handles state-specific labor laws, provident funds, professional taxes, and multi-currency payouts. Multi-entity global payroll routing is standard on the Enterprise 3E tier.
Can we request custom integrations with SAP, Oracle, or NetSuite?
Yes. Our Enterprise 3E tier includes dedicated developer API keys, webhooks, and pre-built bi-directional connectors for major ERPs, financial general ledgers, and identity providers like Okta and Azure AD.

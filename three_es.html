The 3E Approach

Three Pillars. One Platform.

The 3E Framework is how HRGenX organises the entire employee lifecycle — Essentials, Enablement, and Engagement working as a single, intelligent system.

Framework Overview

Why Three E's?

Most HR platforms group features by department convenience. We group by employee journey — because that's what actually matters.

Essentials

The non-negotiables. Every organisation needs to hire people, pay them, track their time, and store their records. Essentials covers all of this — reliably, accurately, and in a single system.

Essentials

The Operational Foundation

Six modules that every organisation must get right before anything else can work.

Core HR

The spine of the platform. Every employee record lives here — personal details, role history, org chart position, documents, contracts, and compliance records. All modules read from and write to this single record, so there's never a data mismatch between what HR sees and what the payroll engine uses.

  • Multi-level org chart with reporting lines
  • Role and grade management with pay bands
  • Document vault with e-signature support
  • Multi-location and entity support

Attendance & Time

Real-time attendance tracking with shift scheduling, leave workflows, overtime calculation, and holiday calendars — all feeding directly into payroll without a manual export or import step. Managers get live dashboards; HR gets compliance-ready records.

  • Shift scheduling and roster management
  • Leave types, accruals, and approval chains
  • Real-time attendance dashboard for managers
  • Direct payroll sync — no manual reconciliation

Payroll Processing

Accurate, compliant payroll runs with built-in tax engines for statutory deductions, reimbursement processing, multi-currency support, and one-click payslip generation. AI pre-checks every run for anomalies before the payment is authorised.

  • Built-in statutory tax and deduction rules
  • Reimbursements and expense integration
  • Multi-currency and multi-entity payroll
  • AI anomaly detection before every run

Talent Sourcing

End-to-end recruitment from job posting to offer letter — without switching tools. Sourcing data flows directly into onboarding so the employee record is already partially built before the person joins.

  • Multi-channel job posting management
  • Applicant tracking with pipeline views
  • Interview scheduling and feedback capture
  • Offer generation and digital acceptance

Onboarding

Structured, automated journeys that cover everything from document collection to equipment provisioning and team introductions — all triggered automatically when an offer is accepted in Talent Sourcing.

  • Role-specific onboarding templates
  • Document collection and e-signature flows
  • Buddy and manager notification sequences
  • Completion dashboards with SLA tracking

Analytics & Reporting

Live dashboards and custom reports spanning all modules — because the data model is shared, there's no ETL delay, no stale exports, and no "the report will be ready tomorrow" conversations.

  • Real-time dashboards across all modules
  • Custom report builder with scheduled exports
  • AI-powered natural language queries
  • Predictive attrition and workforce planning
Enablement

The Development Layer

Four modules that grow people once the foundation is in place.

Performance Management

Goal setting, continuous feedback, structured review cycles, and 360 assessments — all visible to the manager and the employee simultaneously, with the attendance and payroll context that makes the conversation meaningful.

  • OKR and goal-setting with cascade alignment
  • Continuous feedback and check-in workflows
  • 360 assessments with configurable rater sets
  • Calibration grids for end-of-cycle reviews

Learning & Development

A built-in LMS with course creation tools, curated content libraries, skills tracking, and certification management. Learning paths adapt based on performance review outcomes so development is always in response to a real signal.

  • Course builder with video, docs, and quizzes
  • Skills matrix and competency tracking
  • Certification management with expiry alerts
  • AI learning path recommendations

Asset Management

Track every physical and digital asset assigned to an employee — laptops, access cards, peripherals, software licences — with full allocation history, return workflows triggered on offboarding, and depreciation calculations.

  • Asset register with category management
  • Employee allocation history and audit trail
  • Offboarding return workflows
  • Depreciation tracking and disposal logs

Service Desk

Internal HR and IT ticketing with SLA tracking, auto-routing by category and department, a searchable knowledge base, and self-service resolution — so employees get answers without HR manually responding to every query.

  • Configurable ticket categories and SLA rules
  • Auto-routing based on team and query type
  • Knowledge base with AI-assisted article suggestions
  • Manager escalation and approval workflows
Engagement

The Retention Engine

Three modules that build a culture employees want to stay in.

Community

An internal social layer that knows your org structure — groups by team, role, and location, a recognition wall where peers appreciate peers, polls, events, and celebration moments that make the workplace feel human.

  • Org-aware groups and channels
  • Peer recognition and achievement walls
  • Polls, events, and team challenges

News & Communications

Company-wide and targeted communications that reach the right people at the right time — without flooding inboxes. Department heads publish to their teams; leadership publishes to everyone; AI ensures the message lands.

  • Targeted audience publishing
  • Personalised employee news feeds
  • Read receipts and engagement analytics

Wellness

Wellbeing programs, mental health resources, step and wellness challenges, and burnout risk monitoring that draws from attendance and performance data — so you catch the signal before it becomes a resignation.

  • Wellness challenges with team leaderboards
  • Mental health resource library
  • AI burnout risk monitoring across modules

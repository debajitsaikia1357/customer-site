HRGenX Privacy Policy

We are committed to absolute transparency regarding how customer data, workforce telemetry, and access credentials are encrypted, processed, and protected.

Effective: October 2026 GDPR, SOC 2 & DPDP Aligned Version 2.4

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of HRGenX Inc. ("HRGenX", "we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of personal and technical data when using our human capital management platform, APIs, web interfaces, and customer services (collectively, the "Service").

Key Principle: HRGenX acts as a Data Processor with respect to customer employee records and workforce telemetry, and as a Data Controller solely for customer account administration and billing information.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-reliability human capital management, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Account & Identity Data: Full name, corporate email address, organization name, job title, and authentication credentials (including SSO/SAML tokens).
  • Workforce Telemetry: Attendance clock-ins, shift schedules, biometric device timestamps, leave balances, and overtime logs.
  • Payroll & Financial Data: Salary structures, bank account numbers for salary disbursals, statutory tax identification numbers (PAN, PF, ESI, SSN), and expense claim receipts.
  • Log Data & Diagnostics: Browser headers, IP addresses, session cookies, and audit logs collected for security compliance and fraud prevention.

3. Customer Data Protection & Zero AI Training

We recognize that employee records, compensation packages, and operational configurations represent confidential corporate assets:

  • Zero Foundation Model Training: We never train general AI or machine learning models on customer employee data, performance reviews, or proprietary documents.
  • Encryption at Rest & Transit: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 GCM envelope encryption with hardware-backed keys.
  • Automatic Redaction: Sensitive credentials matching standard regex patterns are automatically sanitized before being persisted into operational logs.

4. How We Use Data

We process information strictly for legitimate commercial and technical purposes:

  • To calculate and disburse payroll, bonuses, reimbursements, and statutory tax filings as configured by your organization.
  • To track real-time attendance, overtime, shift rosters, and approved leave deductions.
  • To power continuous 360-degree reviews, agile OKR goal alignment, and skill development matrices.
  • To enforce role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and compliance audit trails.

5. Data Storage & Security Controls

All customer data is hosted in Tier-4 enterprise-certified cloud infrastructure with geographical isolation in designated regions (United States, European Union, and India). Access to production environments requires hardware-token MFA, strict VPN peering, and zero-trust authorization.

6. Third-Party Subprocessors

We partner with vetted infrastructure and payment providers under strict Data Protection Agreements (DPAs):

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Payment & Banking Gateways: PCI-DSS Level 1 certified banking partners.
  • Transactional Email: SendGrid / AWS SES.

7. GDPR, DPDP Act & Statutory Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including India, the EU/EEA, UK, and California), you possess statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request a complete export of your employee records in standard JSON/CSV format.
  • Right to Rectification: Update inaccurate account details directly via self-service employee portals.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of personal data upon organization workspace termination.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain account records for the active duration of your subscription. Operational audit trails are retained for 90 days by default (up to 365 days on Enterprise tiers). Upon contract termination, all customer records and backups are permanently purged within 30 days.
